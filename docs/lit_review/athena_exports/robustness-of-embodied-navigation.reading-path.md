# Reading path: Robustness of Embodied Navigation

12 papers: a survey first, then the milestones in the order they appeared. From Athena's map built 2026-11-01.

1. **[Threat of Adversarial Attacks on Deep Learning in Computer Vision: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00553)** (2018). Naveed Akhtar, Ajmal Saeed Mian. *IEEE Access*. *(start here)*
2. **[Towards Deep Neural Network Architectures Robust to Adversarial Examples](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.5068)** (2014). Shixiang Gu, Luca Rigazio. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
3. **[DeepFool: A Simple and Accurate Method to Fool Deep Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04599)** (2015). Seyed-Mohsen Moosavi-Dezfooli, Alhussein Fawzi, Pascal Frossard B. *IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings - IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings*.
4. **[Distillation as a Defense to Adversarial Perturbations Against Deep Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04508)** (2015). Nicolas Papernot, Patrick Drew McDaniel, Xi Wu et al. *IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (S&P)*.
5. **[Towards Evaluating the Robustness of Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04644)** (2016). Nicholas Carlini, David Wagner. *IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (S&P)*.
6. **[Adversarial Machine Learning at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01236)** (2016). Alexey Kurakin, Ian Goodfellow, Samy Bengio A. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
7. **[Towards Deep Learning Models Resistant to Adversarial Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.06083)** (2017). Aleksander Mądry, Aleksandar Makelov, Ludwig Schmidt et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
8. **[On Evaluation of Embodied Navigation Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06757)** (SPL) (2018). Peter Anderson, Chang, Angel, Devendra Singh Chaplot et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
9. **[Certified Adversarial Robustness via Randomized Smoothing](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.02918)** (2019). Cohen, Jeremy M, Elan Rosenfeld, J. Zico Kolter. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
10. **[The Many Faces of Robustness: A Critical Analysis of Out-of-Distribution Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.16241)** (2020). Dan Hendrycks, Steven Basart, Norman Mu et al. *IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)*.
11. **[RobustNav: Towards Benchmarking Robustness in Embodied Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04531)** (2021). Prithvijit Chattopadhyay, Judy Hoffman, Roozbeh Mottaghi et al. *IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)*.
12. **[Universal and Transferable Adversarial Attacks on Aligned Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15043)** (GCG) (2023). Andy Zou, Zi Wang, Nicholas Carlini et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
