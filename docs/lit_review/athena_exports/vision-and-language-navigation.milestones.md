# Milestones: Vision-and-Language Navigation

From Athena's map built 2026-11-01.

## 2017

- **[Vision-and-Language Navigation: Interpreting Visually-Grounded Navigation Instructions in Real Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.07280)** (2017). Peter Anderson, Qi Wu, Damien Teney et al. *IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings - IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings*.

## 2018

- **[On Evaluation of Embodied Navigation Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06757)** (2018). Peter Anderson, Chang, Angel, Devendra Singh Chaplot et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
- **[Reinforced Cross-Modal Matching and Self-Supervised Imitation Learning for Vision-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.10092)** (2018). Xin Eric Wang, Qiuyuan Huang, Aslı Çelikyılmaz et al. *IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings - IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings*.
- **[Speaker-Follower Models for Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.02724)** (2018). Daniel Fried, Ronghang Hu, Volkan Cirik et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.

## 2020

- **[Room-Across-Room: Multilingual Vision-and-Language Navigation with Dense Spatiotemporal Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07954)** (2020). Alexander Ku, Peter Anderson, Roma Patel et al. *Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP)*.
- **[Towards Learning a Generic Agent for Vision-and-Language Navigation via Pre-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10638)** (2020). Weituo Hao, Chunyuan Li, Xiujun Li et al. *IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings - IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings*.
- **[VLN↻BERT: A Recurrent Vision-and-Language BERT for Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.13922)** (2020). Yicong Hong, Qi Wu, Yuankai Qi et al. *IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings - IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings*.

## 2021

- **[Airbert: In-domain Pretraining for Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.09105)** (2021). Pierre-Louis Guhur, Makarand Tapaswi, Shizhe Chen et al. *IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)*.
- **[History Aware Multimodal Transformer for Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.13309)** (2021). Shizhe Chen, Pierre-Louis Guhur, Cordelia Schmid et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
- **[Structured Scene Memory for Vision-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03454)** (2021). Hanqing Wang, Wenguan Wang, Wei Liang et al. *IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings - IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings*.

## 2022

- **[Think Global, Act Local: Dual-scale Graph Transformer for Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11742)** (2022). Shizhe Chen, Pierre-Louis Guhur, Makarand Tapaswi et al. *IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings - IEEE Computer Society Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition/Proceedings*.

## 2023

- **[NavGPT: Explicit Reasoning in Vision-and-Language Navigation with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16986)** (2023). Gengze Zhou, Yicong Hong, Qi Wu. *Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence*.

- **[NaVid: Video-based VLM Plans the Next Step for Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15852)** (2024). Jiazhao Zhang, Kunyu Wang, Rongtao Xu et al. *Robotics: Science and Systems*.
- **[NavCoT: Boosting LLM-Based Vision-and-Language Navigation via Learning Disentangled Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07376)** (2024). Bingqian Lin, Yunshuang Nie, Ziming Wei et al. *IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence*.
