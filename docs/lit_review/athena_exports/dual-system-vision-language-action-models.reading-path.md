# Reading path: Dual-System Vision-Language-Action Models

10 papers: a survey first, then the milestones in the order they appeared. From Athena's map built 2026-11-01.

1. **[A Survey on Vision--Language--Action Models for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14093)** (2024). Yueen Ma, Zixing Song, Yuzheng Zhuang et al. *IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems*. *(start here)*
2. **[RT-H: Action Hierarchies using Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01823)** (2024). Suneel Belkhale, Tianli Ding, Ted G. Xiao et al. *Robotics: Science and Systems*.
3. **[NaVILA: Legged Robot Vision-Language-Action Model for Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04453)** (2024). An‐Chieh Cheng, Yandong Ji, Zhaojing Yang et al. *Robotics: Science and Systems*.
4. **[Exploring the Adversarial Vulnerabilities of Vision-Language-Action Models in Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13587)** (2024). Taowen Wang, Cheng Han, James Liang et al. *IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)*.
5. **[$π^{*}_{0.6}$: a VLA That Learns From Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14759)** (RECAP) (2025). Physical Intelligence, Amin, Ali, Raichelle J. Aniceto et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
6. **[Interactive Post-Training for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17016)** (RIPT-VLA) (2025). Shuhan Tan, Kairan Dou, Zhao, Yue et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
7. **[ConRFT: A Reinforced Fine-tuning Method for VLA Models via Consistency Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05450)** (2025). Yuhui Chen, Shuai Tian, Shugao Liu et al. *Robotics: Science and Systems*.
8. **[QVLA: Not All Channels Are Equal in Vision-Language-Action Model's Quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03782)** (2026). Yuhao Xu, Yantai Yang, Zhenyang Fan et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
9. **[QuantVLA: Scale-Calibrated Post-Training Quantization for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20309)** (2026). Jingxuan Zhang, Yunta Hsieh, Zhongwei Wan et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
10. **[RISE: Self-Improving Robot Policy with Compositional World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11075)** (2026). Jiazhi Yang, Kunyang Lin, Jinwei Li et al. *arXiv (Cornell University)*.
